## Satire, parody and caricature

Politique 2027 is a work of humour. The characters are **caricatures of public figures** — French politicians, declared candidates (among them an athlete and a video maker known as the "Cosmic Christ"), a foreign head of state, the wife of the French president, two other video makers and a television journalist — depicted solely in connection with their public life. Their powers, titles and situations are made up for laughs: they are not news and not a statement of the author's opinions.

- **Catchphrases.** Some speech bubbles are public lines that became famous in France ("De la poudre de perlimpinpin", "La République, c'est moi !", "Moi président"…), quoted for what they are: cult lines. The others are made up in the spirit of the character. None is offered as a documentary quotation.
- **Guests.** The two video makers and the journalist appear as guests, and no statement is attributed to them beyond harmless lines. One of the video makers and the journalist question every camp in the same way; the journalist's power ("follow the money") refers to the genre of the TV investigation, not to any particular report. The other video maker publicly took a stand against the far right in 2024, so he only appears against the Right camp: this reflects that public stance, not a bias of the game.
- **Media.** A rolling-news channel appears as a broadcast van, against every camp in the same way. The joke is about the rolling-news format (debates and pundits around the clock); it attributes no stance, statement or fact to the channel or its staff.
- **Current events.** School blockades, burning bins, motorists, farmers, taxes, benefits, police, firefighters and tariffs appear as generic twists in the game. They refer to no specific event, place, date or person, and the game stages no violence: a figure "resigns", a student "goes back to class", a fire "goes out".
- The game is not affiliated with any party, candidate, elected official, institution or public service, and is neither endorsed nor funded by any of them.
- It is not a poll, a political advertisement or a call to vote. The "Polls" gauge is an entirely fictional game mechanic.
- All four camps get the same treatment and all four are playable.
- No photograph, party logo or third-party content is used: the portraits are original drawings.

Any person depicted who feels that something goes beyond the limits of satire can [write to the publisher](https://mbonnici.fr/en/contact). The request will be reviewed and answered as soon as possible.
