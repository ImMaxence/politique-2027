## In short

- No account, no sign-up, no form.
- **No cookie** set by the site, no advertising, no analytics, no tracker.
- The game runs entirely in your browser: **it sends no data** to any server.
- Your progress is saved on your device only.

## Data stored on your device

To remember your progress and settings, the site uses your browser's local storage (`localStorage`). This information never leaves your device and the publisher has no access to it.

| Key | Content | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `politique2027:save` | Chosen camp, unlocked levels, stars, endless-mode record, sound muted or not, game speed, forced landscape on phones or not, chosen difficulty (easy, normal or hard), opponents already introduced ("New!" cards), "home screen" tip dismissed or not, best daily-challenge scores (last two weeks), achievements and the totals they count, music on or off | Until you delete it |

So that the game opens and plays **offline** (in particular once added to the home screen), the browser also keeps a copy of the site's files — pages, scripts, styles, fonts and pictures — in its cache (`Cache Storage`, managed by a *service worker*). This cache only holds the site's public files, no data about you; it is replaced with every new version of the game.

The "Share the result" button makes a picture on your device and hands it to your system's share menu (or saves it): nothing is sent until you pick a recipient yourself. A duel link only holds the night, the camp, the match's draw and a score: nothing about you.

This storage is strictly necessary for a feature you ask for (finding your game and preferences again), so it does not require consent. To remove everything (local storage and cache), clear the "site data" for `politique2027.mbonnici.fr` in your browser settings. The game also works when storage is blocked: progress is simply not kept.

## Cookies

The site sets no cookies. The network provider Cloudflare (see below) may, in rare anti-bot checks, set a technical security cookie that is strictly necessary and exempt from consent.

## Technical logs

The site's server **keeps no access log**: the pages you view are not recorded. Only technical errors are logged; they may include the IP address the request came from, and are deleted automatically through log rotation. This processing relies on the publisher's legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and working (GDPR, Article 6.1.f).

## Hosting and processors

- The site is hosted on a personal server located in France.
- Traffic passes through the network of **Cloudflare, Inc.**, which acts as a firewall and content delivery network. In that role Cloudflare processes visitors' IP addresses, including outside the European Union, under its own data protection commitments (standard contractual clauses and the Data Privacy Framework).

No data is sold, rented or passed on to any other third party.

## Fonts, images and third-party resources

Fonts, illustrations, the characters' photos and scripts are served from the site itself (the photos are not loaded from Wikimedia). Nothing is loaded from Google Fonts, an ad network or any other third-party service.

Links to GitHub and to the author's website lead to sites that apply their own privacy policies.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data. In practice the publisher holds no data that could identify you; the only information about you sits on your device, where you can delete it at any time.

For any question, write to the publisher through the [contact form](https://mbonnici.fr/en/contact). You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/en).
